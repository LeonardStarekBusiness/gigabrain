An *EULER PATH* is a $path$ that uses every edge strictly once.

### How to determine?
A Graph $G$ has an *EULER PATH* if all vertices $V$ *except at most 2* have even degree.

Intuition: A vertex needs a "way out" for every "way in". (Quagmire Lachen)
