AN AVL Tree is a binary search tree with the property that the heights of
the two child subtrees of any node differ by at most one. (= as balanced as possible!)
I.E. The balance factor of every node is -1, 0, or 1.

![[Pasted image 20260817201719.png]]

Perform *left rotation* or *right rotation* on certain nodes based on imbalance: *LL, LR, RL, RR*.
