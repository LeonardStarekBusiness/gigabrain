## BIG-O NOTATION
$f(n)$ is:
$\omega(g(n))$ : Strict lower bound — grows strictly faster than  $g(n)$.
$\Omega(g(n))$ : Lower bound (worst case) — grows at least as fast as $g(n)$.
$\Theta(g(n))$ : Tight bound — same asymptotic growth rate as $g(n)$.
$O(g(n))$: Upper bound (best case) — grows no faster than  $g(n)$.
$o(g(n))$: Strict upper bound — grows strictly slower than  $g(n)$.
