[[BINARY TREES]] are trees where each node has at most two children. [[BINARY SEARCH TREES]] maintain an ordered structure for efficient search. [[AVL TREES]] and [[RED BLACK TREES]] are self-balancing binary search trees. [[2-3 TREES]] and [[B-TREES]] are balanced multiway search trees, commonly used for databases and filesystems. [[HEAPS]] are almost complete binary trees.

The *height* of a node is the longest path from it to a child. Intuitively for a tree: $Height = Levels - 1$

Trees can be traversed *inorder*, *preorder* , *postorder* , *levelorder*.
