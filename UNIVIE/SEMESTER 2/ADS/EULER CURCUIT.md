An *EULER CURCUIT* is a $circuit$ that uses every edge strictly once.

### How to determine?
A Graph $G$ has an *EULER CIRCUIT* if all vertices $V$ have even degree.

Intuition: A vertex needs a "way out" for every "way in". (Quagmire Lachen)

Why -2 ? We don't have to get back to our starting point. The Euler Path must start / end at the vertices with odd degree.
